# What Is DreamCision AI·SCRIBE?

DreamCision AI·SCRIBE is a clinical documentation tool that helps you turn patient encounters into polished clinical notes. Instead of spending time typing after a patient visit, you speak naturally — the app listens, transcribes, organizes your chart data, and generates a structured note ready for your chart.

## How It Works (In Simple Terms)

```
You speak → App transcribes → You add chart data → AI generates your note → You review and copy
```

That's the whole workflow. The app runs in your browser or device, but **your encounter data is stored on the DreamCision server** hosted at **Yarmouth Regional Hospital**—not as the lasting copy on your personal machine. In this deployment, patient data is not sent to external consumer cloud services.

## The Three Main Areas

The workspace is divided into three sections:

**1. Chart Data (left side)** — This is where your raw input goes: voice transcriptions, typed notes, uploaded documents, lab results, imaging reports.

**2. Generated Note (right side)** — This is where the AI produces your clinical note. You review, edit, and copy from here.

**3. Tools (left sidebar + top bar)** — Record, Camera, File Upload, AI Prompts, Encounters, Q&A, and Settings.

## What Makes It Different

- **Hospital-hosted server** — Transcription, storage, and AI-assisted steps run on the DreamCision server at Yarmouth Regional Hospital; your device is the client, not the primary place data lives
- **Voice-first** — Dictate naturally; no special commands needed
- **Smart organization** — Automatically separates current encounter data from historical data
- **Evidence-backed** — Can generate consult comments supported by medical literature
- **Works on your phone** — Full functionality on mobile devices
