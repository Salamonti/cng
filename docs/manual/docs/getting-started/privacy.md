# Privacy, Consent & Your Responsibilities

This section covers what you need to know about patient privacy when using DreamCision AI·SCRIBE. **Please read this before your first patient encounter.**

---

## Nova Scotia's Personal Health Information Act (PHIA)

In Nova Scotia, the **Personal Health Information Act (PHIA)** governs how personal health information is collected, used, stored, and shared. As a healthcare provider, you are responsible for complying with PHIA when using any tool that handles patient information — including DreamCision.

### What PHIA Means for You

- **Collection:** You can collect personal health information only for the purpose of providing healthcare to the patient
- **Consent:** Patients must consent (expressly or implied) to the collection of their health information
- **Safeguards:** You must protect health information with appropriate security measures
- **Access:** Patients have the right to access their own health information
- **Complaints:** Patients can file complaints if they believe their information was handled inappropriately

For the full PHIA legislation, see: [novascotia.ca/dhw/phia](https://novascotia.ca/dhw/phia/)

## Patient Consent for Audio Recording

### What Happens When You Press Record

When you press the **Record** button for the first time in an encounter, the app automatically plays a brief consent audio message:

> *"Recording with patient permission."*

### What You Should Do

1. **Before pressing Record**, inform the patient that you are using a digital tool to document their encounter
2. **Confirm the patient is comfortable** with this — a simple "Is it okay if I record our conversation for my notes?" is sufficient in most routine care situations
3. **If the patient declines**, do not record. You can still type notes manually into the app
4. **Document your consent approach** in your practice according to your professional college guidelines

### What You Should NOT Do

- Do not record without the patient knowing
- Do not use recordings for purposes other than clinical documentation
- Do not share recordings with anyone not involved in the patient's care

## Where Your Data Lives

**Encounter data is stored on the DreamCision server** hosted **on-site at Yarmouth Regional Hospital** (operator facility)—not on your personal computer, tablet, or phone as the system of record. You use the app from your device (usually a web browser); the lasting copies of recordings, text, uploads, and generated notes stay on that hospital-site server.

| Data type | Where it is stored |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Voice recordings | DreamCision server (processed and retained there) |
| Transcriptions | DreamCision server database |
| Uploaded documents | DreamCision server |
| Generated notes | DreamCision server database |
| AI-assisted processing | DreamCision server stack (same deployment—not on your device) |

In this deployment, patient data is not sent to external consumer cloud services, AI providers, or unrelated third parties. If you are unsure how a feature connects to the network, ask your privacy or IT contact.

## Your Responsibilities as a User

### Do

- Get patient consent before recording
- Review every AI-generated note before using it — **AI is an assistant, not a replacement for your clinical judgment**
- Keep your login credentials private
- Use the app on secure, password-protected devices
- Log out when finished, especially on shared computers
- Follow your professional college's documentation standards

### Do Not

- Share your username or password with anyone
- Leave your session open on unattended computers
- Use the app on public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks
- Enter patient data you do not need for the clinical encounter
- Trust AI output without reviewing it — **you are responsible for the final note**
- Use the app to make clinical decisions — it is a documentation tool, not a diagnostic tool

---

## Data Retention

- Your encounter data is stored on the DreamCision server at Yarmouth Regional Hospital (not as the primary record on your own devices)
- You can delete individual encounters at any time from the Encounters panel
- Follow your institution's document retention policies for how long to keep clinical notes
- When you delete an encounter, it is removed from the active workspace
