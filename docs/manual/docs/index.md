# Welcome to DreamCision AI·SCRIBE

**DreamCision AI·SCRIBE** is your clinical documentation assistant. It listens to what you say during patient encounters, helps you organize chart data, and generates polished clinical notes. Your encounter data is stored on the **DreamCision server on-site at Yarmouth Regional Hospital**—your device runs the app, but it is not where the primary copy of patient information lives.

Think of it as a very good medical scribe that never takes a day off.

---

## What You Can Do

| Task | How |
|------|-----|
| **Dictate a note** | Press Record, speak, and the app transcribes everything |
| **Upload documents** | Photos, PDFs, lab results — drag and drop or use the camera |
| **Generate clinical notes** | Pick a note type and let AI draft your note in seconds |
| **Edit and copy** | Edit the output, then copy rich text directly into Word or your EMR |
| **Ask medical questions** | Built-in Q&A with evidence-backed answers |
| **Stay current** | Weekly literature summaries delivered to your workspace |
