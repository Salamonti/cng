# Glossary

**ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition)** — The technology that converts your spoken words into text. Also called "speech-to-text" or "transcription."

**Chart Data** — All the raw information you provide: voice transcriptions, typed notes, uploaded documents, lab results.

**Encounter** — A single patient visit or clinical interaction. Each encounter has its own data and generated notes.

**Encounter Queue** — A waiting area for files (audio, images, PDFs) that are being processed. Shows the status of each item.

**Generate** — The action of asking the AI to create a clinical note from your chart data.

**OCR (Optical Character Recognition)** — Technology that extracts text from images and PDFs. When you upload a photo of a lab result, OCR reads the text from the image.

**Prompt** — The set of instructions given to the AI for generating a note. You can customize prompts to change how notes are written.

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** — A technique where the AI searches a knowledge base (medical literature) to support its answers. Used in Medical Q&A and Evidence-Based Comments.

**Template** — A pre-written prompt that you can edit. Each note type has its own template.

**Transcription** — The text version of what you said during voice recording.
